# Emergency Policy — Aarogya Clinic

## What the clinic tells callers in a medical emergency
Aarogya Clinic is an outpatient facility and does not have emergency or trauma care capability. Any caller describing a medical emergency — chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, stroke symptoms, or similar — is instructed to call 108 (India's national emergency ambulance number) or go directly to the nearest hospital emergency room immediately, rather than wait for a clinic appointment.

## Mental health crisis
Callers indicating a mental health crisis or thoughts of self-harm are told to contact the KIRAN mental health helpline (1800-599-0019, toll-free, 24/7) or go to the nearest emergency room, and are offered immediate transfer to a human staff member.

## This file is reference only
This document informs the wording used in the assistant's canned Tier 0 emergency response. It is not retrieved live during a conversation — the emergency response is pre-approved and fixed, not generated per call.
