# Prescription Refill Policy — Aarogya Clinic

## How refill requests are handled
Refill requests submitted through the clinic assistant are logged and forwarded to the prescribing doctor for review. No refill is dispensed or approved automatically — every request requires a doctor's sign-off before the medicine can be collected.

## Turnaround time
Doctors typically review refill requests within 1-2 business days. The patient will be contacted by phone once a decision is made.

## What the assistant can and cannot do
The assistant can log a refill request with the patient's name, medicine, and prescribing doctor. It cannot confirm approval, cannot advise on dosage, and cannot say a medicine is safe to continue without doctor review.

## Controlled substances
Refills for controlled or scheduled medications are never processed by phone or assistant request — the patient must visit in person.
