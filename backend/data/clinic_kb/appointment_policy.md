# Appointment Policy — Aarogya Clinic

## Cancellation window
Appointments can be cancelled free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. Cancellations made within 2 hours of the appointment, or no-shows, may be marked against the patient's record but do not incur any fee — this is a free community clinic service.

## Rescheduling
Patients may reschedule an appointment any number of times, subject to slot availability, by calling the clinic or asking the assistant.

## Late arrival
Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to wait for the next available slot or rebook, at the treating doctor's discretion, to avoid delaying other patients.

## Booking window
Appointments can be booked up to 30 days in advance. Same-day appointments are subject to availability.
