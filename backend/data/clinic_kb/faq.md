# Frequently Asked Questions — Aarogya Clinic

## What documents do I need for my first visit?
First-time patients should bring a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, driving license, or passport) and any prior medical records or prescriptions relevant to their visit. No referral is needed for general consultations.

## Is parking available?
Yes, free two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking is available in the clinic's basement, accessible from the rear entrance on 4th Cross Road.

## What payment methods are accepted?
Cash, all major debit/credit cards, and UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) are accepted. The clinic does not currently accept cheques.

## Do I need to book an appointment in advance?
Walk-ins are accepted but appointments are strongly recommended to avoid waiting, especially for specialist consultations like Orthopedics and Gynecology which run limited days per week.

## Is there a waiting area for children?
Yes, a small children's waiting area with basic toys and seating is available near the Pediatrics consultation room.

## Can I get a duplicate prescription or past record?
Yes, front desk can issue a duplicate of any prescription or report from the past 12 months. Bring your patient ID or registered phone number.
